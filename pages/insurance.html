The right policy depends on how your business actually works

Two businesses in the same industry can need very different coverage. It depends on whether you have employees, whether anyone drives for work, what your client contracts require and what your landlord asks for.

We start with those questions, then quote the coverage that fits across several carriers, so you’re not buying a policy you don’t need or missing one you do.

You see your options side by side, with pricing, before you commit to anything.

Questions that shape your coverage

  • Do you have employees, full or part time?
  • Do you or your staff drive for work, in your own vehicles or company ones?
  • Do client contracts ask for a certificate of insurance?
  • Do clients rely on your advice, designs or professional work?
  • Do you lease space with insurance requirements in the lease?
  • Do you keep equipment, tools or inventory you’d need to replace?
  • Have you had claims in the past few years?
