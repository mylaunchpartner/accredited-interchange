Payroll is a compliance job, not just a direct deposit

Paying people is the easy part. The work is everything around it: withholding the right amounts, depositing taxes on time, filing quarterly and annual returns, and keeping up when you hire in a new state.

A missed deposit or a wrong filing turns into penalty notices months later, usually when you’re busiest. We take that work off your desk and keep it on a schedule.

You approve the pay run. We handle the deposits, filings and forms that come after it.

Where small-business payroll usually goes wrong

  • Federal and state tax deposits made late or for the wrong amount
  • New hires not reported to the state on time
  • Overtime calculated incorrectly for hourly staff
  • Workers paid as contractors who should be employees
  • No state unemployment account after hiring in a new state
  • Final paychecks issued after the state deadline
  • W-2s and 1099s sent late or with errors
